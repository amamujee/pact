Common questions

How do I create a pact?
Use the /pact command in any Slack channel, or react to a message with your workspace's trigger emoji. A modal will guide you through setting the commitment and due date.

How do I mark a pact as done?
Use the /done command in Slack. Make Pact will show your active pacts and let you complete one or more at once.

How do I manage my subscription?
Use the /pact billing command in Slack to access your billing portal, or email us at hello@makepact.co.

How do I uninstall Make Pact?
Go to your Slack workspace settings, find Make Pact under Installed Apps, and click Remove. You can also email us to request full data deletion.
